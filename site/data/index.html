This is the immutable active release: 676 modeled events. It contains 672 reviewed primary-family assignments and 4 explicit family evidence holds.

Current authored atlas: 540 authored cases and 552 stops. The current profiles, source registry and remaining authoring holds are separate from the unchanged release export.

The bundle includes the modeled corpus, typed score matrix, evidence and event-decision ledgers, reviewed editorial fields, statistical results, sensitivities, and bibliography inventories. Copyrighted source PDFs, browser captures, scratch files, and raw model/coder journals are deliberately not redistributed.

These downloads are tied to the release shown above. The included atlas source preserves its separately maintained display layer. A refreshed atlas or publication date does not alter frozen statistical inputs; corrections not yet incorporated in this release must remain identified as such.
