URLs from 5,875 source occurrences. These are not 1,366 verified unique works.
All 851 older work IDs and the 30-record reviewed pilot are preserved.

review of 30 DOI exceptions and two existing chapter access records. The

`../identity_followup_20260908/typed_metadata_overlay.json`; their primary

typed view and its preferred access links for later metadata corrections.
Source-note locators identify a project note, not a claim that the underlying
passage has just been read.

## Current limits

The effective metadata tiers contain 1,185 unresolved secondary records, 116
earlier deposited identities with a mechanical screen, three earlier corrected
identifiers, 30 preserved pilot reviews, 30 reconciled metadata records with
separate passage limits, one reconciled containing-book record, and one known
article whose identifier remains unresolved. `METADATA_STATUS.json` provides
the machine-readable counts. The manuscript reference split remains 27 cited
works and 14 background entries.

The unresolved Historia identifier belongs to Antonio Espino López's 2012
article, whose [publisher page](https://revistahistoria.uc.cl/index.php/rhis/article/view/10102)
provides working access. Neither of its two published DOI forms was registered
in the observed DOI.org checks. A working URL does not resolve that registry
problem.

The review also identifies the correct journal for Yonas Tesema's Ethiopia
article, a journal article misclassified as a dataset in DataCite, and a Handle
misidentified from a publisher's `/doi/` path. It separates Bresson's chapter
from its containing book despite conflicting publisher metadata. Yale's 2024
formats for Olivelle's *Ashoka* are identified, while the older note's cited
copy and page locators remain an explicit exception.

Metadata identity does not establish a source's support for a historical
claim. Source passages, editions and generic topic labels still require
research. No historical claim, event date, statistical input or evidence hold
is changed by this metadata update. Copyrighted source bodies and private
captures are excluded from this bibliography bundle.
