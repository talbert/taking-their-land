Denbigh change: did people have to leave?

Place and era
Alaska, near the Bering Sea and Arctic coasts. The source gives broad age ranges, not a firm event date.
What the evidence supports
A 2018 state report says a migration with Denbigh culture displaced or replaced earlier people. It leaves that choice open. It also describes later continuity with Choris. Selected Cape chapters now give context. They cover chronology, Birnirk and Ipiutak, with figures and plates. They do not settle the earlier Denbigh claim. The Oxford chapter remains unread because access is blocked. Seek evidence that links a human act to loss of homes, land use or control.
