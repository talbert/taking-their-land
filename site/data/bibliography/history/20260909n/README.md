identifiers, 30 preserved pilot reviews, 363 reconciled metadata records with
separate passage limits, one reconciled containing-book record, and one known

misidentified from a publisher's `/doi/` path. It separates Bresson's chapter
from its containing book despite conflicting publisher metadata. Yale's 2024

copy and page locators remain an explicit exception.

Tsafack's printed issue designation is 2017, with 2018 copyright; the publisher
website separately reports issue publication in 2019 and article publication
in 2022. These dates retain their distinct roles. Breasted's 1906 publication
is separate from the scan's 2015 digitization. The inspected Grant book is
separate from the earlier doctoral thesis.

Metadata identity does not establish a source's support for a historical
claim. Source passages, editions and generic topic labels still require
research. No historical claim, event date, statistical input or evidence hold
is changed by this metadata update. Copyrighted source bodies and private
captures are excluded from this bibliography bundle.
