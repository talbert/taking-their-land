Homes, town control and royal land claims

Occurred October 27, 2026

This update adds 15 source notes and revises three. Thirteen new notes concern the Mississippi valley in early North America. Two concern ancient South Asia. The source list now has 598 notes.

A 1989 report traces changes in homes near Cairo, Nodena and the Ouachita. It also raises questions about refuge, salt access and trade. Soto ordered the release of some principal Pacaha prisoners. It does not say that all captives went free. At Quigaute, his forces used half the town and took men and women after a demand for service. Some place names come from restored text. The text does not show one campaign, dates for each gain, or the fate of residents.

Two Otomi notes on land loss and refuge

Occurred October 24, 2026

Two Otomi research notes draw on one modern Spanish account by Levin Rojo. It appears on a UNAM site. The first reports that Nahua groups displaced Otomi people from better lands near Xilotepec. It remains a regional lead: the affected communities and tracts need to be defined.

The second is a qualified case hypothesis about Conin and families who left Xilotepec to seek refuge from Spanish rule. Known but not yet placeable: a guide to the research list

Work date October 17, 2026 (America/Denver) · Added October 17, 2026 (UTC)

Known but not yet placeable is the public list of research questions. It draws from the project ledger. Each entry names a place and a time span.

Each source entry states what the evidence supports, what remains unknown and the next step. It links to the sources. Some entries are leads. Others have a reviewed source account.

A place on this list is not admission to the atlas roster. It is not a conclusion of forced removal or a model score. A record can stay open or leave the candidate queue after review.
