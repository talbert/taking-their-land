## Background reading

These works were retained in the earlier anchor list but are not cited in the manuscript body. They are separated here without inventing an influence on the project or adding citations merely to retain them.

- Brace, S., et al. 2019. "Ancient Genomes Indicate Population Replacement in Early Neolithic Britain." *Nature Ecology & Evolution* 3: 765–771.

- Sokoloff, K. L., and S. L. Engerman. 2000. "Institutions, Factor Endowments, and Paths of Development in the New World." *Journal of Economic Perspectives* 14 (3): 217–232.
