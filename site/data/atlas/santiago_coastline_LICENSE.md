The extract preserves every native coordinate of the principal island's
108 connected coastline ways (17,531 vertices including closure). The
relation's separate tiny offshore islet is outside this main-island extract.

These present-day physical coastline data provide neutral map context.
They do not describe historical boundaries, affected land, routes, or the
extent of the UNESCO property. They remain under ODbL, separately from the
project's original research data and its Creative Commons license.
