Known but not yet placeable: a guide to the research list

Work date October 17, 2026 (America/Denver) · Added October 17, 2026 (UTC)

Known but not yet placeable is the public list of research questions. It draws from the project ledger. Each entry names a place and a time span.

Each source entry states what the evidence supports, what remains unknown and the next step. It links to the sources. Some entries are leads. Others have a reviewed source account.

A place on this list is not admission to the atlas roster. It is not a conclusion of forced removal or a model score. A record can stay open or leave the candidate queue after review.

Unknown evidence does not mean no harm. The list is not a census of harm or proof that the project found every case.

The page also lists cases that await map locations in a separate section. Those cases already belong to the atlas. Their place limits differ from the open research questions.

The Discovery link in the atlas opens the search report. That report links to this list. The link below opens the list.

This update also repairs marker colors and adds a key. The map keeps the same cases and data.
