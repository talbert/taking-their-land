Slave transport, contract work and island residence

Occurred October 6, 2026

The atlas adds a case on slave transport, contract work and residence in the Mascarenes and Seychelles. It spans 1638 to about 1920. The sources describe selected groups and periods. They do not show that every arrival was forced.

Two links show selected voyages in 1770 and 1807. The points are approximate place references. The arcs are not ship tracks. The departure port for the 1718 arrival in Bourbon is not mapped. Other routes and later fates remain unknown.

Slave transport and contract work remain separate. Some former workers bought small plots. The sources do not give one total for people forced to move, or deaths across this whole case. All eight fixed trait values and the family remain unchanged. Each trait has a source note.
