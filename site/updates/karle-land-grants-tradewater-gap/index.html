Karle land grants and a gap at Tradewater

Occurred October 24, 2026

Three new source notes cover an apparent Woodland occupation gap in Kentucky and village grants recorded at Karle. The Tradewater survey draws the gap from a sample of sites. It does not show forced removal.

Senart reads inscriptions 13 and 19 as linked Karajika-Karajaka grants to monks, with a later royal order. Inscription 14 records a grant of an unnamed village by Somadeva. The texts document grants and orders. Prior rights, loss of those rights and whether the grants took effect remain unknown.

The Awatovi update separates the account recorded by Fewkes from his proposed date and causes. The account reports deaths and forced travel. Fewkes does not establish a death toll, and fire damage alone does not date the attack.
