## What reuse permits

material. For CC material, credit Craig Talbert, identify the work and its
license, preserve supplied notices as required, and indicate changes.

another license allowed by its ShareAlike terms. CC BY data and MIT software

No license requires publication of private edits. An independent study is not
automatically an adaptation simply because it analyzes these data. The grants

## Suggested citation

Craig Talbert. 2026. *Taking their land: A chronological atlas of settlement
and displacement*. Cite the atlas build or statistical release identifier
shown with the material and the access date.
[Project](https://latrian.dyndns.org/takingtheirland/).

For the monograph, cite Craig Talbert, *The Shape of Taking*, the displayed
working-draft version and publication build. For data, cite the release ID
and, when practical, the downloaded file or bundle checksum. These citation
details help reproducibility; they do not add license conditions. Translations
and adaptations should identify their changes rather than imply they are the
unchanged original.

## Updating and preserving releases

The publication builder carries these notices into each new HTML/data build
and the monograph PDF. The downloadable bundle includes this file, the license
texts, third-party notices, and per-file component guidance in its manifest.
Existing frozen statistical files are not rewritten to add headers: these
licenses accompany their unchanged bytes. No archival DOI is claimed until
one is actually issued.
