Burned homes and open source questions

Occurred October 14, 2026

The source list now includes Duckfoot in southwest North America. The author argues that someone burned the homes on purpose. He proposes that residents did this before they left. The cause of departure remains unknown.

The Whistling Elk entry adds an original field report. Excavators found a burned inner roof, a mostly unburned outer roof and floor objects. The authors infer a hasty departure. They propose an attack as one possible cause.

The Seminole entry now uses the same evidence rule as other entries. It seeks a bounded process and a land outcome for an affected group or tract. A named surviving community is not required.
