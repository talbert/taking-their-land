second_pass_v6_675

This is the immutable active v6 release: 675 modeled events. It contains 673 reviewed primary-family assignments and two explicit family evidence holds.

The bundle includes the modeled corpus, typed score matrix, evidence and event-decision ledgers, reviewed editorial fields, statistical results, sensitivities, and bibliography inventories. Copyrighted source PDFs, browser captures, scratch files, and raw model/coder journals are deliberately not redistributed.

Later source-reviewed card and boundary corrections can differ from this frozen release. They await required coding and a successor fit; a refreshed atlas or publication date does not mean the statistical data have been refitted. The included atlas source preserves the displayed layer separately.

Original structured research data: CC BY 4.0, © 2026 Craig Talbert. Original narrative prose and methods documents: CC BY-SA 4.0. Quoted sources retain their own rights. Reuse guidance and exceptions; full notices are also inside the ZIP.
