# Final Editorial Suite disposition

Date: 2026-08-28

Artifact: post-v5 corrected successor candidate and working monograph v1.3

Decision: **PASS AFTER REVISION**

Release recommendation: **PROMOTE WITH QUALIFICATIONS**

Target immutable release: `second_pass_v6_675`

## Completed stages

1. **Fact-Check.** Stage 1 found one substantive defect: seven candidate rows
   carried corrected calibration fields beside overbroad legacy public prose.
   The repair was applied once at the reproducible source in
   `CORRECTION_PLAN.json`, regenerated, and validated. It also narrowed the
   monograph's replication and influence language and fixed the direct source
   paths. Closed at commit `fc3056c4`.
2. **LLM Artifact Tells.** Stage 2 found no hard artifact or source-integrity
   defect. It converted three reader-facing editorial commands to declarative
   historical prose and removed two minor administrative-sounding phrases.
   The candidate and manifests regenerated and validated without a numerical
   change. Closed at commit `400f6a47`.
3. **Calibration Review.** Stage 3 audited 43 substantive claim groups: 24
   historical, 13 statistical-interpretive, and six monograph groups. All 43
   are Matched; there are no Overclaimed, Underclaimed,
   Unsupported-but-sound, or Uncheckable verdicts inside the declared local
   evidence boundary. No target edit or downstream retrigger is required.

This three-stage chain is the sole final review authorized for the finite
post-v5 correction cycle. It is complete and will not be recursively rerun.

## Release qualifications

Promotion is authorized only with all of the following preserved:

- The immutable successor contains 675 active events, 748 total corpus rows,
  73 inactive audit rows, 60 dimensions, and 40,500 score/NA cells.
- `atlas-indo-aryan-migration` remains an inactive audit-preserved wrapper. Its
  migration evidence is not erased, but it does not count as a bounded active
  land/resident-outcome event.
- The old year-bearing Nipur-Pasate and Nimme/Tummu-Habhu IDs remain durable
  aliases to chronology-neutral successor IDs.
- The 117 source-adjudicated cell changes across nine retained events remain
  exactly as ledgered: 104 `U`, one structural `NA`, and twelve numeric values.
- The primary-family distribution remains: conquest and incorporation 170;
  expulsion or flight without replacement 179; land clearance for state or
  project use 25; low-state durable expansion 44; settler replacement 111; and
  state-directed demographic remaking 144. The Croton and partial Paeonian
  return rows remain explicit family evidence holds.
- Both immutable v5 and the successor retain nine factors under canonical
  preprocessing. Their nine-dimensional maximum principal angle remains
  2.765 degrees. Forced eight moves 6.685 degrees and rotates named-axis
  allocation. The public eight-trait card remains an evidence-aware editorial
  summary, not eight newly fitted latent factors.
- None of the nine leave-one-corrected-event-out scenarios crosses the declared
  materiality thresholds. This does not isolate each correction from the
  effect of omitting its event.
- Equal-era weighting, era-median imputation, KNN imputation, structural-zero
  imputation, documentation subsets, and alternative event boundaries remain
  material limitations and must travel with release interpretation.
- Normalized mechanism tags remain internal after their earlier reliability
  gate failure. The separate public mechanism descriptions are historical
  prose, not a newly validated normalized mechanism taxonomy.
- Statistical inclusion is not automatic atlas inclusion. The public atlas
  may add or update corrected cases only after ordinary authoring, map, source,
  trait-card, and settled-frame validation.

## Authorized claims

The project may say that the source-adjudicated roster and cell corrections do
not materially change the canonical nine-dimensional factor space; that a
forced-eight fit reallocates boundary-sensitive axes; that no one corrected
event omission crosses the declared influence thresholds; and that every
changed event now has a traceable disposition and calibrated public account.

The project may not say that the corpus is complete, every boundary is uniquely
correct, coverage is bias-free, the six families are natural kinds, exactly
eight traits were statistically discovered, or the Editorial Suite freshly
reverified every original historical publication.

## Stop rule

This final disposition closes the current Editorial Suite. A future review is
targeted only to a later edit that changes a load-bearing claim, a new
statistical release after v6, or evidence that contradicts a controlling
adjudication. Ordinary authoring, map geometry, metadata refreshes, and
deployment checks do not reopen this suite.
